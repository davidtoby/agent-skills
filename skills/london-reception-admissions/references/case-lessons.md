# Originating process and lessons / 原始过程与踩坑

Case reviewed: 13 November 2026. This is an anonymised learning record; school-specific rules are historical examples, not universal defaults.

## 实际过程

1. 确认目标是 2027 年 9 月 Reception，读取学校官网的 2027/28 Admissions Policy 和 SIF。
2. 起草英文询问信，说明已阅读文件，集中询问参观、新搬迁家庭的教会出席记录及补充材料。
3. 学校办公室要求电话联系。家长实际打电话确认了 11 月 13 日上午 9:45 参观，并要求起草感谢和确认邮件。
4. 家长提供牧师支持信，并准备校长交流问题；信件存在不等于已满足完整录取条件。
5. 参观当天提交多段校方讲话，涉及阅读／Phonics、行为规则、家校合作、支持资源与招生程序。
6. 对转录中的截止日期、简化录取顺序、拒绝 offer 后续和师资比例做了区分与核验。

未取得 CAF 提交回执、SIF 收件确认、录取通知或 offer 接受证据。因此不能把准备和参观写成申请已完成。

## Observed sequence

The family established September 2027 as the target, read the school's year-specific policy and SIF, drafted an enquiry, followed the office's request to phone, confirmed the visit, obtained clergy support and shared the tour speech for interpretation. Submission receipts and an offer were not established.

## Observed pitfalls / 已观察到的坑

| Pitfall / 坑 | Evidence and correction / 依据与修正 |
|---|---|
| Wrong borough / 找错 borough | Home was Brent; the school was in Westminster. The policy said to complete the home-authority CAF, while returning the school's SIF separately. / 申请地按住址 authority，学校补充表单独提交。 |
| Same-name school / 同名学校误命中 | Searches returned other St Luke's schools in Islington, Lambeth and elsewhere. Match the school's official address and identity before using a policy. / 不仅凭校名选择搜索结果。 |
| Visit treated as interview / 参观被当作面试 | The originating policy said the headteacher visit provided information and played no part in admission decisions. / 先读会面用途，不靠表现推定录取。 |
| Wrong date from ASR / 录音日期错误 | The supplied transcript said “February 28”; the supplied 2027/28 policy stated 15 January 2027. Preserve the conflict and check the annual official timetable. / 不直接将口述转录作为截止日期。 |
| Categories compressed incorrectly / 录取类别被错误压缩 | Earlier explanations omitted the looked-after priority and/or non-faith sibling category, producing a misleading category number. The originating policy had seven criteria; the same-church/no-sibling route was criterion 3, not criterion 2. / 完整保留正式顺序和编号。 |
| Letter substituted for SIF / 牧师信代替 SIF | The actual SIF contained a minister-only section asking personal knowledge and attendance over the preceding year. / 检查谁必须填哪一部分，询问支持信是否还需配合表格。 |
| Recent mover automatically eligible / 新搬迁家庭被自动判为符合资格 | The policy could consider previous-church attendance, but did not establish this family's eligibility. / 只陈述真实记录，向学校核实缺口。 |
| One preference or rejection myth / 志愿与拒绝 offer 误解 | A tour statement suggested rejection would lead to the next school. That was not verified. Neither listing one school nor declining an offer should be presented as a way to force allocation. / 按 council 的实际程序处理。 |
| Staffing turned into guarantee / 师资说法变成保证 | A teacher plus two TAs was reported at the visit. A calculated adult/child ratio was not a fixed staffing promise. EAL is not automatically SEN. / 记录当场说法，另问语言支持。 |
| Draft assumed sent / 草稿被当作已发送 | The record showed requests for drafts and a family-confirmed phone booking; not an email-send receipt or application confirmation. / 每一步用实际证据标状态。 |

## Source routes to re-check / 下次核验入口

- [Originating school's application page](https://www.stlukesprimary.org.uk/page/?title=Apply+to+our+school&pid=28) — follow its policies link and choose the intended academic year. / 跟随政策链接，确认学年。
- [Government school register: originating school](https://www.get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/Establishments/Establishment/Details/101134) — identity/address check. / 身份核对。
- [Brent Council](https://www.brent.gov.uk/) — navigate to primary admissions and the current normal-round guide. / 查询居住地的当年流程。
- [London eAdmissions](https://www.eadmissions.org.uk/) — use only after checking the relevant council route. / 不凭通用门户替代 borough 指引。
