# London Reception Admissions

Turn school information, correspondence and visit notes into an evidence-backed application plan and concise English/Chinese communication. This skill covers London, England; do not extend its rules to the whole UK.

For the origin and observed mistakes, read [case-lessons.md](references/case-lessons.md) when reviewing conflicting dates, admissions criteria or visit transcripts. Use [application-brief.md](assets/application-brief.md) for a reusable handover document. User-facing introductions: [中文](README.zh-CN.md) / [English](README.en.md).

## Establish the case

Use information already supplied; ask only for missing details that affect the route.

- Exact date of birth and desired September entry year; confirm the normal cohort against the relevant council guidance. Do not infer it from a child's approximate age or the current date alone.
- Home borough, actual residence, target school's full name, address and official website. The residence authority and school authority may differ.
- Normal Reception round, in-year transfer, independent school, EHCP route, or a request for a different year group. Do not apply normal-round instructions to these other routes.
- Which materials exist: official policy for the target year, school SIF, correspondence, faith evidence where relevant, and visit notes.

Keep identifiable family information in the user's private application materials. Public examples use placeholders; never put addresses, dates of birth, clergy letters or private chat links into a reusable skill.

## Verify before interpreting

Read the target school's official admissions policy **and the actual form** for the correct academic year. Find the council's current normal-round timetable and portal. Confirm the school's identity using its address and, when needed, the government's school register; many schools share the name St Luke's.

Build a small evidence table: claim, source URL/file and section, applicable year, checked date, and any unresolved conflict. Label information as official rule, reported school statement, family fact, or interpretation. Search snippets, earlier assistant answers and a tour recording are leads, not substitutes for the governing policy.

Check each deadline separately: CAF, SIF, supporting evidence, offer response and any later appeal/waiting-list request. School pages may point to a separate policies page. If an annual document is missing, say which year was found and ask the school/council the specific unresolved question; do not silently reuse another year's deadline.

## Keep application routes separate

- **CAF / council application:** follow the home borough's procedure, including cross-borough preferences. Check its portal; London commonly uses eAdmissions.
- **SIF / school supplement:** determine whether it is required for the claimed category, which sections apply, who signs, and where/when it is returned. A school form or an enquiry email does not replace the CAF.
- **Supporting evidence:** a minister's letter does not automatically replace the minister-only section of a SIF. Inspect the form before asking for a generic recommendation letter.

Record preferences in the family's genuine order. Explain the local allocation rules from current guidance. Do not claim that listing one school improves its chances, that first choice guarantees a place, or that declining an offer automatically produces the next preference. Confirm the council's rules before advising on offers, late applications or waiting lists.

## Evaluate criteria without inventing eligibility

Preserve the policy's complete oversubscription order, category numbers and tie-break rules. A spoken summary may omit looked-after children or non-faith sibling categories. Do not renumber categories from that summary.

For a faith route, check whose attendance counts, the named church/denomination, required frequency and look-back period, sibling condition, clergy knowledge/signature and treatment of recent movers. Describe a possible category conditionally until the school confirms the evidence. Do not invent earlier church attendance or treat a letter as proof that the entire period is satisfied.

Distinguish an additional-language learner (EAL) from special educational needs (SEN). Ask about classroom support without diagnosing SEN or claiming a guaranteed staffing ratio. A tour's reported teacher/TA numbers describe that visit, not every class or every time of day.

## School contact and visit

Match the school's requested next step. If the office asks for a phone call, provide a short call script; after the family confirms a booking, draft an acknowledgement with the exact date, weekday, local time and attendees. Do not mark a drafted email as sent or a proposed tour as booked.

A school tour/headteacher meeting is not necessarily an admissions interview. Use the policy to establish its purpose. For the originating school, the policy explicitly said the information visit played no part in deciding admission; do not prescribe coaching a child to win a place. Confirm any different assessment route from the school itself.

Keep an enquiry focused on what remains unknown: for example a recent-mover attendance period, whether clergy must complete the SIF, and the separate submission routes. Do not ask the school to repeat rules the family already has.

Review visit transcripts for material errors before translation. Dates such as “February 28” that conflict with the year's written policy stay marked as disputed until checked. Correct obvious ASR errors with context; do not turn garbled speech into new rules. Preserve uncertainties around offer rejection, staffing and eligibility.

## Deliver and track

Produce the requested language(s), with:

1. A short case/route summary and source-backed deadlines.
2. An action table: task, owner, due date, destination, evidence and status.
3. Only the needed enquiry/call/visit/follow-up text.
4. Explicit open questions and a useful next step.

Use observable stages: draft prepared, booking confirmed, visit attended, CAF submitted, SIF received, evidence acknowledged, offer received, offer accepted. Submission needs a receipt/reference or equivalent confirmation for **each** route. Attendance and a positive visit do not prove submission or an offer. If rejection or waiting-list advice is requested, check the current policy before proceeding.

Drafting does not authorize external messages or applications. Send, upload or submit only within the user's explicit current authorization; reuse existing authorization and verify the actual result rather than requesting it again. Do not create reminders or contact clergy/schools simply because this skill is invoked.
